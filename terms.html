You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for the activity performed through your account. Provide accurate profile information, especially when using academic personalization features.

3. Uploaded documents

You should only upload documents that you have permission to use. Do not upload material that violates copyright, privacy, confidentiality, or applicable law.

4. AI-generated answers

ChatWithPDF may use AI models to generate answers from uploaded documents. AI output can be incomplete or incorrect. Always verify important academic, legal, medical, financial, or professional information against reliable sources.

5. Student profile

Course, branch, year, and related academic details may be used by the application to tailor explanations. These details should be treated as context rather than proof of a user's academic status.
