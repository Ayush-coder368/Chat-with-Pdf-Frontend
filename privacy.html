Privacy Policy

This privacy page is a frontend project template. Before production use, replace it with a policy that accurately reflects your actual database, hosting, authentication provider, AI provider, analytics, and retention practices.

1. Information we collect

The application may collect account information such as name and email, optional academic information such as course and branch, uploaded document metadata, conversation history, and questions submitted to the AI service.

2. How information is used

  • To authenticate users and maintain accounts.
  • To display uploaded documents and conversation history.
  • To provide AI answers based on uploaded documents.
  • To personalize explanations using optional academic context.
  • To maintain and improve application reliability and security.

3. Uploaded PDFs

Documents may need to be transferred from the browser to your backend and then to the Python AI service for extraction and retrieval. Your final production policy should clearly identify where files are stored, how long they are retained, and which third-party AI providers can process their contents.

4. Academic information

Course, branch, year, and college information is optional personalization data. The application should only use it for purposes explained to the user and should provide a way to update or remove it.

5. Local demo storage

This frontend demo uses browser localStorage for simulated account, document, and chat data. This is not a secure production authentication or database mechanism. Replace it with server-side sessions or secure token-based authentication before deployment.

6. Security

A production version should use HTTPS, secure password hashing, protected API routes, authorization checks, secure cookies where applicable, file validation, upload limits, malware scanning, and appropriate secrets management.

7. Your choices

Users should be able to update their profile and, where supported by the backend, delete their uploaded documents and conversation history.
